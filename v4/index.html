Before you reserve

Frequently asked questions

How do I reserve?

Book a 3D virtual tour and choose your apartment with us. £3,000 then takes it off the market: £2,500 counts towards your 20% deposit and £500 is BRIK Studios' admin fee.

Can I buy with a mortgage?

Yes. Most buyers use a 75% buy-to-let mortgage, arranged in the months before completion. We can introduce you to our mortgage broker.

What do I pay, and when?

£3,000 to reserve. The rest of your 20% deposit within 28 days, on exchange. The balance on completion, expected early 2028.

What are the lease and ground rent?

A 999-year lease with nil ground rent, and a 10-year build warranty.

What are the running costs?

Service charge est. £2.50 per sq ft per year, about £86 a month for an average 1 bed. Letting management is 10% of the rent. Both are in every number here.

Who finds the tenant and manages it?

We do, for 10% of the rent. You don't need to live nearby.

Can I see it before I buy?

It's under construction, so we take you round our 3D model of the finished building on a video call. St Marys Gate next door is a finished Hall & Co scheme you can see for yourself.

When is completion?

Early 2028. You'll get notice to complete well ahead of the date.

W. Harvey, prize medal saddler, archive
The building's heritage

Named for its neighbour.

Charter House is named for Stockport's Market Hall, right next door. Harvey Street is named after William Harvey, a prize-medal saddler and leather goods merchant whose shop also kitted out Stockport's Boy Scouts.

The building borrows its identity from his adverts: the scout on the keyring comes straight from them.
