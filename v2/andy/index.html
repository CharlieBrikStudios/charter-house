03 — HeritageCharter House · Investment report

The building's heritage.

Charter House is named for its neighbour, Stockport's Market Hall, right next door. Harvey Street is named after William Harvey, a prize-medal saddler, harness maker and leather goods merchant.

Harvey's shop on Heaton Lane sold brief bags, Gladstone bags, travelling rugs and driving aprons. As RV's Saddlery Stores, it also kitted out Stockport's Boy Scouts with bell tents, axes and canteens.

Charter House borrows its identity from those adverts. The scout on the keyring, tote and cap comes straight from them.

Why the UnderbanksConservation area since 1974

Huge demand, tiny supply.

A conservation area since 1974, recently awarded a £1.8m National Lottery heritage grant, and fast becoming the place to be, full of independents, restaurants, cafés and bars.

The real win is supply. Listed buildings and strict planning mean nothing over four storeys can be built in the conservation area, so future supply has a hard cap: a tiny fraction of places like Salford Quays.
